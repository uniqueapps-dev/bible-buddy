How to Use

HomeYour daily verse and quick session entry. Tap Continue to begin. An Insight card appears once you have 3+ journey entries.
ReadLoad any Bible passage. Use Inquire, Explain, Apply, and Pray to explore it.
StudyDeep dive — explanation, cross-references, application, and personal notes.
ListenHear the passage read aloud. Use Play, Pause, and Stop.
MeditateQuiet timer with breath animation. 1, 2, or 5 minutes. Chime signals the end.
PrayerWrite what's on your heart and receive encouragement. Record answered prayers.
JourneyA log of your readings, prayers, notes, and meditations.
ProfileSet your name, API key, model, and system controls.
